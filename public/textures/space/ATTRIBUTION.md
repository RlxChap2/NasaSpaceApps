# Sky assets

NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio / Ernie Wright.
The local JPG is the 1024 × 512 preview of the catalog-derived map, not the 4K EXR. Used faintly for background and environment reflections.

Individual star positions, magnitudes and B–V color indices: D3 Celestial stars.6.json, https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial . Copyright Olaf Frohn; BSD-3-Clause. See CATALOG-LICENSE.txt.

5044 measured catalog entries are rendered as point sprites. Sky orientation and exposure are artistic choices; the scene is not an observer-location or live-time sky chart. No random stars or atmospheric twinkling are added.
